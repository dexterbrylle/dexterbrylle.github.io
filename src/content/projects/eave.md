Eave puts a note or checklist where you'll see it: your iPhone Lock Screen. Tick off a checklist item or clear a pin when you're done. The note stays saved so you can use it again.

## Fit a note into your day

Schedule a pin for later, keep notes visible with Home Screen widgets, or pin a note through Siri and Control Center. Papers, fonts, and cats let you make the note your own.

## A personal place for reminders

Your notes stay on your phone, with no account required. Eave is coming soon to the App Store. Its website includes an interactive preview you can try now.
